1. **Primary endpoint preserved:** `Q*_{i,m}(K)=max_{1..K} raw_RGB8_PSNR` computed using GT **only after** sample generation; all K physical trajectories charged for end-to-end compute. It measures *candidate-generation possibility*, NOT automatically obtained deployed reconstruction. Report executable GT-free selection separately. 180° ambiguity-aware PSNR is an auxiliary analysis, raw oriented PSNR the user-chosen principal metric.
2. **No Xue overlap claim** without full paper access. Title/DOI only raises novelty risk. No negative intellectual-property assertion.
3. **FAST-DIPS paper timing is explicitly per run**, despite reporting best4 quality; multiplying all methods by 4 yields a more useful portfolio cost without changing its relative speed ratios. Other hardware, model checkpoints and even GT PSNR representation mean cross-paper 29.253 vs NP's 30.388 is *not* a paired comparison. That is the exact reason a fair common experiment (if the method-paper claim survives) needs a new shared source/observation/metric protocol.
