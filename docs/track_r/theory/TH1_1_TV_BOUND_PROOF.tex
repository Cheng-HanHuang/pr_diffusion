\section{Total-variation cost of symmetry breaking}
\label{app:tv-defect}

\begin{theorem}[Gaussian-chain equivariance-defect bound]
\label{thm:th1-tv-proof}
Let \(G\) be a finite orthogonal group, fix \(g\in G\), and suppose
\(X_T\sim\mu_T\) where \(g_\#\mu_T=\mu_T\). For \(i=T,\ldots,1\) assume
\[
 X_{i-1}\mid X_i=x\ \sim\
 N(m_i(x),s_i^2 I_d),\qquad
 s_i>0,
\]
where \(s_i\) does not depend on the current state. Set
\(\Delta_i^g(x)=m_i(x)-g m_i(g^{-1}x)\).
Assume the sum of expected squared standardized defects below is
finite. If \(L\) is the law of \(X_0\), then
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(L,g_\#L)
 \le \left[
 \sum_{i=1}^{T}\frac{
 \mathbb E\|\Delta_i^g(X_i)\|_2^2}{4s_i^2}
 \right]^{1/2}.
\]
In particular, for any measurable \(B\),
\[
 |L(B)-L(g^{-1}B)|
 \le \left[
 \sum_{i=1}^{T}\frac{
 \mathbb E\|\Delta_i^g(X_i)\|_2^2}{4s_i^2}
 \right]^{1/2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(P\) denote the joint path law of
\((X_T,X_{T-1},\ldots,X_0)\), and let \(Q=g_\#P\),
where the action rotates every path coordinate. Since the initial
distribution is invariant, \(P\) and \(Q\) start from the same law.
Orthogonality of \(g\) implies that the conjugate transition law under
\(Q\) is
\[
 Q_i(\,\cdot\mid x)=N(g m_i(g^{-1}x),s_i^2 I_d).
\]
The chain rule for relative entropy gives
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\|Q)
 = \sum_{i=1}^{T}\mathbb E_P\left[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}\left(
 N(m_i(X_i),s_i^2 I_d)\|
 N(gm_i(g^{-1}X_i),s_i^2 I_d)
 \right)\right].
\]
For Gaussians with common covariance \(s_i^2 I_d\), the conditional
KL divergence is
\(\|\Delta_i^g(X_i)\|^2/(2s_i^2)\). Consequently
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\|Q)
 =\sum_i\frac{\mathbb E_P\|\Delta_i^g(X_i)\|^2}{2s_i^2}.
\]
Projection onto the final state is measurable, and its output laws
are \(L\) and \(g_\#L\). Data processing for total variation followed
by Pinsker's inequality (with
\(\mathrm{TV}(P,Q)=\sup_B|P(B)-Q(B)|\)) implies
\[
 \mathrm{TV}(L,g_\#L)
 \le\mathrm{TV}(P,Q)
 \le\sqrt{\tfrac12D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\|Q)},
\]
which gives the constant \(1/4\) in the asserted square root.
The event statement is the definition of total variation.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Observable lower bound on transition defects]
\label{cor:th1-necessary-defect}
Under the hypotheses above, if a measurable \(B\) has
\(\eta=|L(B)-L(g^{-1}B)|\), then necessarily
\[
 \sum_i \mathbb E\|\Delta_i^g(X_i)\|^2/s_i^2
 \ge 4\eta^2.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Square the TV inequality and multiply by four.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Important interpretation limit.}
The corollary lower-bounds the sum of standardized
\emph{transition-mean equivariance defects}; it does not, without
a solver-specific stability bound, directly lower-bound the
denoiser's relative equivariance defect measured on noised clean
images. For a differentiable denoiser inside a gradient-through-network
update, the relevant mean defect can also involve its Jacobian.
Therefore a denoiser-only profile on noised images is a descriptive
empirical proxy and must not be substituted into the theorem's
trajectory expectation without a justified coupling or distribution
shift estimate.

\begin{remark}[State-independent compatible covariance]
\label{rem:th1-covariance}
The result extends to transitions with positive-definite
state-independent covariance \(\Sigma_i\), provided
\(g\Sigma_i g^\top=\Sigma_i\). The corresponding bound is
\[
 \mathrm{TV}(L,g_\#L)
 \le\frac12\left[
 \sum_i\mathbb E\,
 (\Delta_i^g(X_i))^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}\Delta_i^g(X_i)
 \right]^{1/2}.
\]
If covariance depends on state or fails to commute with \(g\),
its KL terms cannot generally be discarded.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Deterministic microsteps and terminal readout.}
The Gaussian-chain theorem can accommodate a fully deterministic
inner map \(H_i(X_i)\) inside a mean \(m_i\) that is followed by a
nondegenerate Gaussian innovation, provided the resulting conditional
law is exactly the specified Gaussian shift. A stochastic Langevin
inner loop requires representing each Gaussian update separately;
additional optimizer state may require an augmented Markov state.
In the latter case, a deterministic \emph{equivariant} terminal
readout may be applied after the last stochastic state without
increasing TV. An arbitrary deterministic non-equivariant terminal
map cannot be handled this way.

\paragraph{Counterexample to an unrestricted deterministic readout.}
Let \(g(x_1,x_2)=(x_2,x_1)\), let
\(Z\sim N(0,I_2)\), and consider the chain whose only noise step has
equivariant mean \(m\equiv0\). Its defect sum is zero. Now apply the
non-equivariant deterministic final map
\(H(z)=(\max(z_1,z_2),\min(z_1,z_2))\).
The resulting output lies strictly in the half-plane
\(x_1>x_2\) almost surely; its \(g\)-pushforward lies in
\(x_1<x_2\). Their TV distance is one despite the zero defect
of the earlier Gaussian step. This demonstrates why a final
non-equivariant denoiser or choice among inner minimizers cannot
silently be omitted from the hypotheses.

\paragraph{Family-specific interpretation (conditional).}
For DAPS, include every noisy Langevin microstep and re-noising
operation in the path; the exact-inner-sampling proposition in
DAPS does not show the implemented chain is posterior-exact.
For SITCOM, the inner optimization map and its state need
equivariant initialization and update/tie rules, especially because
the denoiser may be differentiated.
For FAST-DIPS, the projection, JVP/VJP or finite-difference
probe, step-size selection, and backtracking must preserve the
action; its pre-existing local-Gaussian KL analysis has a
\emph{different} reference pair and does not imply this
orientation-TV theorem. These checks are structural conditional
statements, not claims that the native code was fully audited.

\paragraph{Primary methodological predecessors.}
Pinsker's inequality; the KL chain rule for Markov path measures;
the equal-covariance Gaussian KL identity; and equivariant-kernel
propagation (GeoDiff, Xu et al., 2022; Hoogeboom et al., 2022)
are standard. The application being tested here is an explicit
per-channel Fourier-symmetry mismatch along a conditional inverse
sampler. No new general information inequality is claimed.
