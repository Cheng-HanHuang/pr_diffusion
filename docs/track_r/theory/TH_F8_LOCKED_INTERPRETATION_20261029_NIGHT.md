**Selection and generation.** On the primary F8 panels, v1 race covers **21/28** reported points: E0 **7/7**, E1 **5/7**, raw oracle **5/7**, orbit oracle **4/7**. Exchangeable-rank S2 covers **22/28**, including all **14/14 E0/E1** points. Therefore F8 does **not** favor race over S2 by the locked interval-coverage rule, unlike SITCOM B16. DAPS's historical prefixes have very few CONSISTENT failures (B22 **0/200**, DEV80 **18/320** runs); with so few wrong-but-passing competitors, both residual selectors can succeed often. Yet the models do **not** become equivalent: pooled K8 v1 race versus S2 predicts E0 **.9428/.9021** (4.1-point gap) and repaired E1 **.9631/.9434** (2.0-point gap). The observed E0/E1 are **.928/.939**. Sparse CONSISTENT decoys reduce selection disagreement, especially after orientation repair, but do not prove exchangeable ranks.

The v3 orbit oracle **.9344 [.9195,.9510]** is closer and covers the observation, but its full-panel coverage is only **17/28**, below v1 race **21/28** and S2 **22/28**. Better calibration of one oracle does not justify promoting v3 after unblinding.

**Stopping and flags.** The independent historical-prefix H2 forecast, committed about five hours before CP1, predicted **+2 to +4** equal-compute points (central **+3**); actual gain was **+3.38**: stopping E1 **.931** at **3.195** runs versus fixed E1 **.897**. However, the approximations predicted only **2.835** runs and stop/fixed levels **(.957,.930)** or **(.993,.953)**. Gain accuracy partly reflects cancellation of upward biases in both success levels, not calibration of those levels. H3 also fails: **5/11** FLAG_C images are unsolved in the observed eight-run panel; this does not certify intrinsic impossibility.

**Conclusion:** F8 supports the frozen stopping advantage while exposing overoptimistic generation, near-convergence (not identity) of repaired selection, unsuccessful overall v3 transfer, and limited FLAG_C precision. All negative results belong in the paper.
