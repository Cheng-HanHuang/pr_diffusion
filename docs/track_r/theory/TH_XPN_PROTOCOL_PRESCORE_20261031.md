# XP-N image-disjoint ImageNet stopping hypotheses: prospective mechanism note

**Registered before receiving any XP-N candidate or protocol output (2026-10-31).** This is a prospective *mechanism-based* prediction for a new image-disjoint panel, not a resampling of the 100-image D1/XP-D4 cohort. No XP-N run has been used. The protocol and its equal-mean-runs comparator remain frozen. The independent planner A16.5 population-transfer intervals are context only, not input to this calculation.

**N-H1a (Nmax=16, gain at least -1.0 percentage point): predict PASS.** Central mechanism estimate **+0.2 percentage point**, approximate judgment range **[-1.8,+2.2] pp**, low-to-moderate confidence. The first two threshold-passing candidates can be found quickly for many ImageNet images. On the previously released ImageNet D1-P2 16-run panel the gain was +0.3 pp. Since adaptive stopping and equal-mean-runs fixed K use similar small budgets at N=16, a much more negative difference is not a primary expectation, but differences in the new image mix could falsify this.

**N-H1b (Nmax=64, gain below -1.0 percentage point): predict PASS.** Central mechanism estimate **-2.7 percentage points**, rough range **[-5.5,+0.7] pp**, low confidence because the tail of image-specific passing rates is poorly identified. The already released 100-image XP-D4 panel's gain changed from near-zero at N=8 to -3.4 pp at N=64. The exact stop-at-M law predicts E[T|s] approximately N when s is much smaller than 1/N, so even a few near-never-passing images absorb a large portion of the mean budget. Their own protocol quality may remain poor, while equal-mean-runs fixed K spends extra runs on all other, solvable images. The released 4--6 hard-image XP-D4 budget concentration (about 43% of generated runs) is evidence of the mechanism, not an estimate of the new panel's hard-image count.

**Not a law of nature.** If the new image-disjoint panel has almost no near-zero-passing images, or if the extra runs are productive on its hard images, N-H1b could fail. If it has a larger concentration of such images, N-H1a could be negative as well. Neither structural unsolvability, true pass probability zero, nor the new panel's exact difficulty distribution is identified from four runs per image. The numerical ranges above are transparent subjective predictions, not frequentist confidence intervals, and were not fitted from XP-N outcomes.

**Scoring:** preserve these two signed forecasts and compare with the preregistered frozen protocol's 95% image-bootstrap summaries and equal mean runs after the XP-N results are released. Do not change this note after a score is obtained.
