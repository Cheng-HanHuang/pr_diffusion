## Binding restrictions

- no direct NP-prefix → SITCOM-suffix revival in the first screen;
- no LF early → DAPS late relabeling (that is LF-v1);
- no hidden best-of-k, retry, shared-prefix credit, or ground-truth selector;
- no controller/threshold mining on B21/B22 images;
- no fifth donor family;
- no adaptive schedule, large panel, Track B, or Track C without amendment and authorization.

B23.2 can be constructed only from B23.1-qualified donors. If no cross-family donor qualifies, the
planner chooses between a narrowed DAPS-native claim, revision, or pivot; this template does not
manufacture an alternative.
