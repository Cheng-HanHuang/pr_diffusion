Fresh2’s B21/B22 100-image panel, B22 baseline panel, and inspected failure taxonomy are historical
evidence and exposure—not method-selection data for B23.

Reviving direct NP-to-SITCOM handoff requires a separate written hypothesis that identifies a
material difference from B3–B8, includes the historical implementation as a control, and receives
planner approval. It is not available in B23.0 or the empty B23.2 template.

DiffStateGrad or any fifth external module family is also outside the initial scope. Literature
motivation does not establish compatibility with nonlinear Fourier phase retrieval.
