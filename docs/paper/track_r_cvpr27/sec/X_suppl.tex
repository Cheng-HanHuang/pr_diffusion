\section*{Supplementary material (outline)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=S\arabic*.]
\item Proofs: \cref{lem:orbit}, \cref{prop:ratio}, \cref{cor:blind} (likelihood-only weights and pruning; why full twisted-SMC weights are not covered), \cref{thm:equivariant}, \cref{thm:tv}, and the posterior--sampler triangle bound. \item Full audit tables per solver, cohort, metric and $K$; ImageNet at secondary thresholds.
\item The noise floor: $\rho/\sigma$ and the fit-error/absorbed-noise decomposition per class and solver; the data-term calibration runs (XP-K).
\item Controls: noise level; $512$ canvas; DPS; PC-DAPS and DiffFPR on the same measurements; the published DAPS configuration on FFHQ (if run).
\item Orientation tests on ground truth and reconstructions, both datasets; equivariance-defect profiles.
\item Exploratory: ground-truth-free reliability diagnostics (cross-run agreement, PRTF-style score) and the mixed-twin anatomy of inconsistent failures.
\item Compute: timings, GPU-hours, and the compute frontier.
\end{enumerate}
