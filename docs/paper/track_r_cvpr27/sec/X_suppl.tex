\section*{Supplementary material (outline)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=S\arabic*.]
\item The GT-free protocol: the frozen specification, its hash and freeze time; the reference implementation; development results on every panel (FAST, DAPS and SITCOM prefixes; ImageNet phase 1); the frontier over $M$ and $\tau_S$; the label-free threshold; every held-out hypothesis with its outcome; secondary panels (DAPS-4K FFHQ and ImageNet, SITCOM ImageNet, FAST face-CLIP $\Eone$). \pending{theory: when stopping beats fixed $K$ at equal budget (heterogeneous consistency rates); optional registered forecast of the protocol (v3P).}
\item Symmetry: the orbit lemma (classical; proof for the even canvas), the posterior ratio within an orbit, orientation-blindness of measurement-only selection and likelihood-only weights (and why full twisted-SMC weights are not covered), invariance of the output law under equivariant samplers, the total-variation bound by the equivariance defect, the posterior--sampler triangle bound; a per-solver source audit of every operation other than the denoiser; equivariance-defect profiles.
\item Selection model: derivation of \cref{eq:curves}, the residual-race model and its fit, the order-statistic estimators and their unbiasedness, tests of (S1)--(S2), the four-moment non-identifiability example, and the data-specific identification bounds.
\item Frozen forecasts: files, hashes, commit times; every forecast against its observation (v1, v2, v2 E1, v3); the composition-conditional selection test.
\item Full tables per solver, cohort, metric and $K$; the per-metric oracle; ImageNet at secondary thresholds.
\item The noise floor: $\rho/\sigma$ and the fit-error/absorbed-noise decomposition per class and solver; the data-term calibration runs (XP-K).
\item Controls: noise level; $512$ canvas; DPS; PC-DAPS on the same measurements.
\item Why a blur or misalignment cannot break the twin ambiguity ($|\cF(x*k)|=|\cF x|\,|\cF k|$), and what masks and references change.
\item Compute: timings, GPU-hours, and the compute axis of the protocol.
\end{enumerate}
