\section{Symmetry of the operator and orientation preference}
\label{sec:suppl-symmetry}
For $s\in\{0,1\}^3$ let $R_s$ rotate color channel $c$ by $180^\circ$ inside the $256\times256$ frame whenever $s_c=1$, and let $\cG=\{R_s\}$.
\begin{lemma}[exact symmetry; classical]
\label{lem:orbit}
For the operator of \cref{M-eq:operator}, $\cA(R_s x)=\cA(x)$ for all $x$ and all $s$.
\end{lemma}
On the even canvas, a $180^\circ$ rotation of the centered frame is index negation followed by a one-pixel cyclic shift; both leave DFT magnitudes unchanged.
This is the twin ambiguity of~\cite{hayes1982reconstruction,fienup1986stagnation}, applied per channel.
