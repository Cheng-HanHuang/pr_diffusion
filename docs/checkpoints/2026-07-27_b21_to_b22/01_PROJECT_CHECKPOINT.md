## 2. Adopted method

For each locked phase-retrieval measurement:

1. run two independent full DAPS trajectories;
2. use `ann400`, `diff5`, LF disabled, HIO disabled;
3. initialize the selected candidate as trajectory 1;
4. replace it by trajectory 2 iff

   ```text
   exact_loss_2 < exact_loss_1 - 0.7
   ```

5. return the selected candidate.

This is called **Fresh2**. It uses no clean image, PSNR, learned failure detector, or post-hoc oracle at runtime.

## 4. Failure decomposition

## 5. Rejected, superseded, and diagnostic paths

These results must be preserved because they justify the final simple policy and prevent repeated work.

### Equal-cost allocation

- A second independent full DAPS restart beat the LF-guided second arm on the completed allocation panel.
- Matched timing showed LF and a fresh full run had effectively equal wall cost.
- Fresh2 became the preferred equal-cost allocation.

### Third and fourth restarts

- Fresh3 looked promising on the development budget curve.
- On a disjoint validation panel, Fresh3 added only one success beyond Fresh2 and improved only one of twenty images.
- Fresh3 was rejected as the default; blind Fresh4/Fresh5 scaling was stopped.

### HIO warm starts

- HIO replacement candidates underperformed ordinary DAPS.
- A later HIO auxiliary arm added a few rescues but was concentrated on very few images and had excessive total cost.
- HIO is preserved as a forensic mechanism, not a default policy.

### LF guidance

- LF guidance produced real rescues but lost to a same-cost fresh restart.
- It is not the default second arm.

### Continuation/branching

- Continuation state capture was technically validated.
- Equal-cost branch-from-step-200 allocation underperformed fresh independent restarts.

### Conditional failure detection

The following clean-free triggers were rejected for runtime fallback:

- raw exact-loss threshold: excellent within-panel ranking but poor cross-panel calibration and excessive flagging;
- measurement-norm-normalized residual: poor validation AUC and missed persistent failures;
- relative two-run features, including rotation-aware disagreement: useful diagnostic ranking but insufficient precision/selectivity.

Do not weaken the frozen detector gates after seeing these results.

## 6. What may and may not be claimed

### Supported claims

- Two independent DAPS trajectories with a clean-free exact-loss margin selector substantially reduce catastrophic single-run failures on the prospective FFHQ panel.
- The selector matches the two-candidate oracle on the completed panel.
- Remaining raw-PSNR failures split into known 180-degree ambiguity cases and genuine candidate-generation failures.
- The genuine failures are visually heterogeneous rather than one repeated attractor.
- Additional blind restarts, tested warm starts, LF allocation, continuation branching, and tested fallback detectors did not justify replacing Fresh2.

### Unsupported or premature claims

- Fresh2 is superior to SITCOM or NP on the same locked measurements.
- Fresh2 is state of the art.
- The method generalizes unchanged across noise levels, datasets, or measurement operators.
- The auxiliary 95/100 ambiguity-aware result is deployable at runtime.
- The five persistent failure categories define validated causal mechanisms.
